# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

MCP Gateway welcomes technical disagreement and direct engineering feedback. Contributors are expected to keep discussion focused on the work and treat other participants with respect.

Constructive participation includes:

- critiquing ideas, code, and evidence rather than people;
- explaining trade-offs and uncertainty;
- accepting that maintainers may reject scope or architecture changes;
- giving others room to ask questions and correct mistakes;
- avoiding harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, or sustained personal attacks.

## Scope

This applies in project-controlled spaces including issues, pull requests, discussions, review threads, and community channels operated for the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior materially disrupts the community or threatens participants.

For conduct reports involving sensitive information, use a private maintainer/security contact path where available rather than posting private details publicly.

## Engineering disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is allowed.

Statements such as “this migration can lose data” or “this abstraction increases coupling” are appropriate when supported with reasoning. Personal attacks, ridicule, and bad-faith escalation are not.

Maintainers should apply these rules consistently regardless of contributor status.
